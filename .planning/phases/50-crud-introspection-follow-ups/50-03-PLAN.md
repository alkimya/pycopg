<objective>
INTRO-F02 — add materialized-view introspection to `db.schema.*`: `materialized_views()`
(names, mirroring `views()`) and `materialized_view_columns()` (per-view columns in the exact
8-key shape as `table_info`), on BOTH `SchemaAccessor` and `AsyncSchemaAccessor`.

Purpose: `information_schema` (views AND columns) fully excludes materialized views
(live-confirmed on `pycopg_test3`), so matviews are invisible to the v0.9.0 surface. Dedicated
`pg_catalog` queries (`pg_matviews`, `pg_attribute`/`pg_class relkind='m'`) close that gap while
keeping the frozen surface minimal (names + columns only, D-15).

Output: two `queries.*` constants + two methods (both accessors) + `test_schema_v110_surface`,
docs rows, mock/injection/live tests. Self-contained — touches no CRUD surface.
</objective>
